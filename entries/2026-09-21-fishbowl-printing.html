Fishbowl Printing System

September 21, 2026 · Week 4 · Field Study to Prototyping

Field study details
Location System Observed
Fishbowl Computing Lab MPRINT 9/21/26, 10:45 AM

1. Field Work

On Monday, we observed how students use the MPRINT system in the Fishbowl. We followed the experience from entering the lab to choosing a printing method, submitting a job, and finding the correct printer.

2. What We Noticed

  • There are three printing areas, and they do not all offer the same printing options.
  • Students can choose different ways to print, including the MPRINT website, mobile printing, or releasing a job at a printer.
  • Detailed guides are available near some printers, and the info/help desk can also provide support.
  • Wayfinding is less clear. There are few signs that help a first-time user understand where each printing area is located or which one they should use.
  • Most students we observed seemed to know where to go, which may be because they had used the space before.

4. From Observation to Prototype Ideas

Based on our observations, I would focus on improving the wayfinding around the Fishbowl printing areas.

  • Add a simple layout/map of the Fishbowl near the entrance showing where each computing site is located.
  • Add a large, visible sign at each computing site so students can identify the printing area from farther away.
  • Add signs explaining what type of printer is available at each site, such as:
    • Black-and-white printing
    • Color printing
    • Letter-size printing
    • Tabloid-size printing
  • Make this information visible before students walk to a printer, so they can choose the correct computing site more easily.
  • Keep the same information consistent between the entrance map, signs, and MPRINT system.

5. Reflection

The MPRINT system itself provides detailed printing options, but the physical wayfinding in the Fishbowl could be clearer. Most students we observed seemed to already know where to go, but a first-time user may have difficulty knowing which computing site has the printer they need. My prototype ideas focus on making that information more visible before students reach the printer.
